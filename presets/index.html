Frequency claims, sorted by what can be measured

A number can be real and still not prove the story attached to it. This table separates the acoustic question — is the tone there? — from claims a spectrum cannot test.

How to read this. “Measurable” means the workbench can show a peak at that frequency and estimate how much of the signal time it is present. It says nothing about health, mood or spiritual effects, and the tool deliberately does not pretend otherwise.

Every measurable entry opens the workbench with the target and a ±3 Hz tolerance. A steady tone needs a tolerance of 1–3 Hz; a “432 Hz track” claim may need 1 Hz if it is precise.
