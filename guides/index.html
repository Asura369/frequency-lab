What a spectrum can actually tell you

Six practical explanations, written for the questions people bring to this tool: tuning claims, “miracle” frequencies, hum, binaural beats, and the oldest mistake in audio — mistaking the loudest partial for the pitch.

A note on claims. These guides are deliberately conservative. They explain what the measurement shows and refuse to upgrade a tone into a medical or spiritual effect. If a claim cannot be tested with a microphone and an FFT, it is labelled that way instead of being quietly ignored.
